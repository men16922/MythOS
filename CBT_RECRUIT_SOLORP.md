## Why this sub (and the risk)

r/Solo_Roleplaying = people who run TTRPGs alone with **oracles, GM emulators (Mythic GME,
Ironsworn/Starforged, One Page Solo Engine), journaling, emergent "no-myth" play**. They are
*exactly* the audience for an AI-narrated solo RPG — **but** the community is **divided on AI**:
some use LLMs as an oracle/GM, others are firmly against AI-generated prose/art on ethics +
quality grounds. So:

- **Transparency about AI is mandatory**, not optional (disclose up front — non-disclosure reads
  as bad faith).
- **Lead with the differentiator** that this crowd respects: **combat is a deterministic engine
  the LLM can't touch** — directly answers their "LLMs hallucinate / can't be trusted with rules"
  distrust.
- Position MythOS as a **tool/experience for solo play**, never *"an AI that replaces the GM."*

## Framing — DO / DON'T (from community-reception research)

**DON'T say** (these trigger backlash):
- "AI Dungeon Master" / "the AI is your GM" / "replaces the GM"
- "infinite content / endless adventures" (reads as AI-slop)
- "writes better than a human" / any quality-superiority claim
- hide or soft-pedal that it's AI

**DO say** (these earn goodwill):
- "an LLM **narrates**; a **deterministic engine** resolves combat — it **cannot override** range,
  cover, initiative, or dice"
- name the **limitations** honestly (it's a beta; LLM has no perfect memory — that's why there's a
  session-memory ledger + loop carry-over)
- frame it as **a tool that does the bookkeeping so you keep the agency**
- use their vocabulary: *oracle, GM emulator, emergent, prep-light, actual-play journal, run/loop
  carry-over = campaign continuity*

**Body:**

Hey r/Solo_Roleplaying 👋

Upfront and honest: **this uses an LLM for the narration**, so if AI-driven prose isn't your thing,
no worries — totally fair, scroll on. But I built it specifically to fix the thing that makes me
(and a lot of you) distrust "AI as GM": **the LLM never touches the rules.**

In **Project MythOS**, an LLM plays the *narrator/oracle* of a cyberpunk city and reacts to the
choices you actually make — but the moment a fight starts, control hands off to a **separate,
deterministic tactical engine**: range, cover, elevation, initiative, FOCUS, dice. The AI **can't
override it, can't fudge HP, can't forget initiative.** Story is improvised; the fight is consistent
and fair.

**How it plays as a solo experience (~30–60 min loop):**
- No prep. You connect, pick an archetype, and the narrator sets the scene.
- Every choice bends **stability** and **pursuit** — the world keeps state and reacts (a lightweight
  causality ledger, not just freeform text).
- Combat drops into a **grid tactical map** (skills, allies, cover/elevation) — pure rules, no LLM.
- A loop ends with a real outcome; **what you saved/lost carries into your next run** (campaign-style
  continuity, not a reset).

It's basically the GM-emulator + oracle role handed to an LLM, with a **hard rules layer underneath**
so the mechanics stay honest.
