Hey r/Playtesters 👋

I'm looking for **5–10 players** for **round 2** of the Project MythOS closed beta.

It is a single-player browser RPG where an AI narrates the story live based on your choices, but combat transitions to a separate, strict rules-based tactical grid engine (no AI hallucinations or fudging).

**Combat got a major overhaul since round 1:**
* 🎯 **Aim previews** — hit % and damage forecasts up front; pushes and blasts draw their landing spot and exact blast tiles before you commit.
* ⚔ **Enemy telegraphs** — see what each enemy will do next turn (which tiles, how hard) on your turn.
* 🔥 **Status warfare** — burn, corrode, freeze, shock, and hacking an enemy to attack its own side. Enemies inflict them too.
* 💥 **Terrain play** — throw grenades at chosen cells; shove enemies into walls and structures for bonus slam damage.
* Direct command of up to **4 companions**, plus a full visual overhaul of the combat board and cut-ins.
