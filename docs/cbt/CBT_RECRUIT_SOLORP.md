**World:** You are a glitch (an "unregistered signal") hunted by a city-wide AI in a dystopian cyberpunk Neo-Seoul.

Currently working:
* **The AI handles the story/roleplay** (acting as your Oracle, improvising cyberpunk scenes and choices live).
