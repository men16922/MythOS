## Shared Style & Aesthetic Guidelines
To maintain visual consistency across all generated clips, ensure your video generation model aligns with the following baseline style:
* **Style**: Cinematic Y2K digital cyberpunk, highly detailed realism, subtle digital glitches, and CRT scanline effects.
* **Palette**: Deep navy-blue shadows contrasted with vibrant cyber-cyan, electrical copper, and gold neon lights.
* **Atmosphere**: Wet, rain-slicked surfaces, heavy steam, volumetric fog, and ambient lens flares.
