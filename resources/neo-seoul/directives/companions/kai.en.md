## KAI_COUNTING_STARS (affection=1, flags=kai_found, image=scenes/kai_dream_fragment.png)
title: The Machine That Counts Stars
---
Kai dims every panel on the abandoned subway platform until it resembles a night sky. Pointing to seventeen stuck pixels, he asks, "Do humans name stars they cannot see?" When you say yes, Kai saves one under your name. Even after the system warns that the next loop will erase the data, he presses save again. "Disappearing is not the same as never having existed."
