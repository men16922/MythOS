## LIN_YUE_LEDGER_MARGIN (affection=1, flags=met_lin_yue, image=scenes/lin_yue_secret_request.png)
title: Margin of the Ledger
---
After the night market closes, Lin Yue deletes one line of your debt from her holographic ledger. "Don't mistake this for charity. I miscalculated today." Yet beside the erased entry she has written the exact time you returned alive. She folds the display and looks away. For someone who trusts only transactions, concern is an asset with no account to hold it.
