## SERIN_FIRST_LIGHT (affection=2, image=characters/se-rin.png)
title: Flickering Trust
---
Se-rin pauses beneath a failing neon sign. With the pursuit fading one block behind, she looks directly at you for the first time. “You’re lasting longer than I expected.” A trace of caution leaves her voice. The synthetic coffee she offers is lukewarm, but it is the first thing anyone in this city has given you without asking for payment. She turns back toward the dark, standing one step closer than before.

## SERIN_PROMISE (affection=4, flags=trusted_se_rin, image=characters/se-rin-biker.png)
title: Echo of a Promise
---
Se-rin kills the bike’s engine and removes her helmet. The Spire’s light cuts a cold outline around her. “If I don’t recognize you in the next loop,” she says, choosing each word, “leave this somewhere I can find it: I won’t disappear before you.” In a world where memory resets every loop, it may be a promise she cannot keep. She still holds out her little finger—an oath that cannot be backed up as data or code.
