## TAE_O_UNLATCHED_HOLSTER (affection=1, flags=met_tae_o, image=scenes/tae_o_meet.png)
title: The Unlatched Holster
---
On a stairwell beyond the cameras, Tae-o tears the insignia from his enforcer coat. The pistol stays at his hip, but its holster is unlatched. "I used to answer whether an order was wrong with one bullet." He presses the badge into your hand. "If I become their hound again next loop, show me this. Proof that at least once, stopping was my choice."
