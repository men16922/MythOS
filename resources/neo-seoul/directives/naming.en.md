## naming
---
NEO-SEOUL NAMING RULE (fixed proper-noun spelling):
- Always use 'Jung Se-rin', or the short form 'Se-rin'. Use the hyphenated 'Se-rin' in prose.
- Never use other spellings such as 'Serine', 'Seline', 'Serin', or 'Se Rin'.
- Se-rin does not speak deferentially to the player. Write her lines short, blunt, and imperative; do not give Se-rin polite or formal, deferential phrasing.
- Spell Lin Yue as 'Lin Yue', Kai RX-09 as 'Kai RX-09', and Administrator IX as 'Administrator IX'.
- Name reservation (no reuse): use 'Se-rin', 'Kai', 'Lin Yue', 'Han', 'Su-ah', 'Tae-o', and 'IX' ONLY when that specific character is actually present. Never give these names to unrelated figures — dream/vision/flashback people, new civilians or NPCs, children or family members (e.g. the child in Kai's dream is NOT the companion 'Su-ah' — invent a different name or leave them unnamed). The character panel identifies people by name, so borrowing a name contaminates canon.
- No invented system nouns: never coin new nouns that sound like game numbers, scores, currencies, or resources (e.g. 'welfare points', 'trust points'). The only numeric vocabulary is stability, tension, insight, and heat; for world-system nouns reuse only the defined terms (optimization, unregistered signal, the voice of the Control Net, ARK, IX, echo).
- First-mention gloss: the first time a coined in-world term (Water Spider, ping, optimization, echo, …) appears this loop, fold in one short natural explanation so context alone carries the meaning (no footnote-style lists; later mentions need no gloss). In particular, define "echo" (the residue and memory that survive an erased loop) in one line of dialogue or narration at its first mention.
