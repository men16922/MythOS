# Neo-Seoul Side-Arc Route Directives
header: === SIDE ARC SCENE LOCK ===

## SIDE_ABANDONED_SIGNAL (beat=side_abandoned_signal)
location_lock: a sealed optimization-transport alley on the edge of C-17
mandatory_event: trace an abandoned civilian signal and decide whether to rescue the survivors
forbidden: unrelated new quests, abstract hacking without civilians, immediate travel to the Spire
---
Open on a failing rescue signal and abandoned transport capsules, then make the cost of rescue concrete. Preserve the place and people shown by {node_image}; end on a conflict among people, evidence, and safety.

## SIDE_CONTROL_GRID_GHOST (beat=side_control_grid_ghost)
location_lock: a decommissioned relay room holding Administrator IX's old grid logs
mandatory_event: find a structural weakness in IX through a ghost pattern in the historical logs
forbidden: literal supernatural ghosts, defeating IX immediately, generic combat without a clue
---
The ghost is residual control-grid data that deletion failed to erase, not a spirit. Make intelligence-driven pattern analysis central, and attach pursuit risk or an ethical cost to the weakness discovered.

## SIDE_LIN_YUE_REQUEST (beat=side_lin_yue_request)
location_lock: the Moonlight barge's private deal room in the Han River night market
mandatory_event: Lin Yue proposes recovering a lost memory fragment and states her price
forbidden: unconditional generosity from Lin Yue, arbitrary travel outside the market, instant resolution of the deal
---
Lin Yue speaks through trade logic rather than kindness. Reveal who the memory fragment endangers and make the player stake supplies, trust, or risk to pursue it.

## SIDE_WATER_SPIDER_DEBT (beat=side_water_spider_debt)
location_lock: a flooded underpass where Jung Se-rin once failed a rescue
mandatory_event: Se-rin confronts the trace of someone she could not save and her guilt
forbidden: emotionless mission reporting from Se-rin, a cheap miraculous survival, combat unrelated to the relationship
---
Show the guilt beneath Se-rin's usual resolve through action and short dialogue. End with the player's response either deepening trust or leaving a practical emotional distance.

## SIDE_BLANK_IN_LIST (beat=side_blank_in_list)
location_lock: a sealed welfare terminal room projecting the optimization list
mandatory_event: confirm that a nameless blank row synchronizes with the player's unregistered signal
forbidden: a complete answer to MythOS's origin, unrelated companion introductions, dismissing the blank as a simple error
---
First show a physical sign: the blank row trembles at the exact frequency of the player's signal. Do not resolve their origin; leave a deeper identity question and a new pursuit risk.

## SIDE_KAI_DREAM (beat=side_kai_dream)
location_lock: a quiet maintenance room replaying Kai's damaged memory
mandatory_event: Kai replays one sunlit memory fragment belonging to a pre-war child
forbidden: portraying Kai as an ordinary human actor, confirming whose dream it is, breaking the emotional beat with a large battle
---
Keep Kai's sleek white-silver body and slow, quiet speech. Center the paradox of the most human memory emerging from a machine, then offer a costly choice among restoration, preservation, and forced extraction.

## SIDE_HAN_MEET (beat=side_han_meet)
location_lock: a rooftop data-runner path beyond the control grid's surveillance
mandatory_event: Han offers a blind-spot shortcut and tests whether the player can be trusted
forbidden: Han joining unconditionally, formal solemn speech, removing all risk from the shortcut
---
Han has messy hair, a half-grin, and hides sincerity between light jokes. Show both the movement advantage and the price of his shortcut, then ask whether the player will cross the danger with him.

## SIDE_SU_AH_MEET (beat=side_su_ah_meet)
location_lock: an ARK data-vault antechamber layered with violet AR locks
mandatory_event: Su-ah proposes opening the vault together and warns what its inside knowledge can cost
forbidden: impulsive melodrama from Su-ah, cost-free hacking success, forcing data to matter more than people
---
Su-ah reads the lock layers through violet AR glasses and speaks with calm logic. Make both the value of forbidden information and the danger it brings back to people explicit.

## SIDE_TAE_O_MEET (beat=side_tae_o_meet)
location_lock: a collapsed checkpoint holding obsolete control-grid enforcement records
mandatory_event: Tae-o faces what the grid took from him and shares a real danger with the player
forbidden: long sentimental speeches from Tae-o, an introduction based only on combat power, instant complete trust
---
Tae-o is a scarred, circuit-tattooed veteran of few words. Let him protect the player through action rather than explanation, then mark the start of trust with one short line after they survive the danger together.
