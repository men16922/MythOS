Account and data deletion

MYTHOS — Signal Run (세계:접속) · Developer {{DEVELOPER}}

Delete in the game (Android app or web)

  1. Open the game. Without the app, sign in with the same Google account on the web version on this site.
  2. While signed in with Google, open Settings with the gear icon.
  3. Under your profile, press Delete account.
  4. Read the notice and press Delete for good.

Request without the app

If you cannot reach the game, email {{CONTACT_EN_INLINE}} with the Google email you signed in with. After confirming it is you, we delete the account within 30 days and tell you when it is done.

What is deleted

  • Cloud saves (Archive progress, the run in progress)
  • All finished-run records
  • Daily Signal leaderboard entries (display name and results)
  • Monthly AI spend and the Signal+ subscription record (cancel the subscription itself in Google Play)
  • Your Google sign-in record (account id, email, name, photo in Firebase Authentication)

Server data is erased immediately and cannot be recovered.

What is kept

  • Server access logs (IP, the anonymous id, …): kept up to 30 days for security, then deleted automatically.
  • Crash reports (Android, not linked to your account): kept up to 90 days, then deleted automatically.
  • Daily AI-call counts and reported AI lines (kept 90 days): stored without any player id, so they are not linked to your account.
  • Progress on your device: it lives only there and goes away when you uninstall the app or clear the browser's site data.
